Abstract

ZecPrivacy ($ZECP) is a privacy-focused community digital asset on Solana, inspired by the principle that financial privacy is a fundamental right.

This document describes the project vision, token details, live PumpSwap market, planned roadmap and risks. Privacy tooling is planned for future development.

02

The problem

Public blockchains expose balances, transfers and counterparties. Salaries, savings and commercial relationships can be traced. Privacy tools often remain disconnected from the ecosystems people use.

03

Our vision

ZecPrivacy aims to bring a privacy-first culture and tooling to Solana, combining confidentiality with low fees and fast settlement. $ZECP represents participation in this community and is intended to support future tooling.
